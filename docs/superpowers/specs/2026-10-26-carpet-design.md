# Carpet marketplace design
React + Vite frontend based on the supplied screenshot. White compact marketplace header, location and product search, breadcrumbs, filter chips, six-column product grid, large rug illustrations, prices, specifications and supplier details. Tablet and mobile adapt to fewer columns. Use fictional CarpetMart branding and entirely dummy suppliers and local SVG carpet illustrations. Search, city/category filters, grid/list toggle, carousel, details, call and enquiry dialogs work locally. Forms clearly confirm demo-only submission, with no network requests or personal data storage. No backend, authentication or payments.
